🔬 Gas Exchange Review
External — At the Alveoli
O₂ → alveoli to blood
CO₂ → blood to alveoli
Process = diffusion
Internal — At the Cells
O₂ → blood to cells
CO₂ → cells to blood
Result = ATP (energy)
